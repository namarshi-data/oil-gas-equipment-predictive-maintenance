Why 99.1% precision still misses failures

Precision and recall trade off against each other, and the deployed threshold is tuned to minimize maintenance-policy cost, not to maximize either metric alone.

  • The cost-selected threshold trades recall for fewer visits.
  • Daily labels differ from event prevention.
  • Abrupt trips have no learned warning signature here.
  • Same-generator testing does not establish field transfer.
Cold-start fixtures have 0% recall. They trigger commissioning review, not an assurance that equipment is healthy.

Guarding against a silent partial batch

A batch job that returns the expected row count can still have silently dropped one asset and duplicated another, so row counts alone are not a sufficient check.

  • Validate unique, complete source-row coverage.
  • Check asset/date identity and finite risk scores.
  • Fail candidate validation before changing the default.
  • Keep quality flags separate from the model's alert.
