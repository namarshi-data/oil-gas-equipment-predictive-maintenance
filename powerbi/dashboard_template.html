Why visits and caught events can differ

A fixed-schedule visit before evaluation can prevent an early-period failure. The visit remains in the audit, but the period does not pay for it again.

Operational false-alarm rate = unnecessary charged visits / charged visits. Classifier FPR = FP / (FP + TN). A no-visit policy has an undefined service rate, shown as a dash.

From telemetry to an auditable decision

Synthetic sensors→Causal SQL cleaning→Chronological ML scoring→Policy replay→Facts + dimensions→PBIP / TMDL + companion
Readings · asset / day

One daily score, shared by all policies. Never multiplied by three.

Events · event / policy

One failure opportunity under each alternative policy. Compare policies, never sum them.

Visits · asset / policy / visit

All dispatches, including unnecessary, unsuccessful and carry-in visits.
